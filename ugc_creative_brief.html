01

Brand Context & Objectives

About Nouslib

Nouslib is an open-minded and welcoming dating app designed to help people meet without judgment, based on their own desires, preferences and expectations. The app is inclusive, private and built with user safety at its core.

Nouslib is new to the Japanese market and brings a touch of French dating culture and the French approach to relationships. This “French touch” can be explored creatively, but it should not necessarily become the sole focus of the video.

Users can register alone or as a pair and connect with people in the way that feels right for them.

Nouslib Features & Terminology

  • Send a “Kiss” to show someone you’re interested in them.
  • Send a “French Kiss”, similar to a Super Like.
  • Make video or audio calls directly through the app or website.
  • Leave comments and recommendations on the profiles of people you’ve met or previously spoken with.
  • Share personal stories publicly or exclusively with close friends.
  • Create private photo albums and choose exactly who can access them.

Content Instructions

  • The in-app content we provide is available for you to use freely in your videos. These videos and screenshots use fictional profiles, so the content can safely be used.
  • At the end of each video, you must include the Nouslib logo / app icon with a Japanese equivalent of “Download Nouslib.”
  • Subtitles must always be included. Content should also respect Nouslib’s visual identity, particularly through subtitles using our typography and brand colors.
  • PNG illustrations are available to make content more dynamic — provided separately.
  • Overall tone: warm, welcoming and dynamic. Safety and privacy are core to the Nouslib experience and should come through clearly.

03

Platform Guidelines

Very important

Meta and other platforms heavily moderate explicit sexual content.

Please avoid
  • Nudity
  • Explicit sexual vocabulary
  • Overly suggestive visuals
  • Adult-oriented messaging
Focus on
  • Genuine connections
  • Privacy
  • Safety
  • Community
  • Freedom to meet people your own way
  • Trust

The objective is content that feels authentic and native to social media while remaining fully compliant with platform guidelines.

04

Video Formats to Produce

We would like to test four main creative formats.

TYPE 1

POV / Storytelling

Native UGC — the creator shares a personal story (real or scripted) directly to camera, speaking naturally as if talking to a friend.

TYPE 2

App Demonstration / Review

A mix of face-to-camera footage and screen recordings demonstrating the app’s interface and features. Goal: show Nouslib is modern, premium and trustworthy.

  • Beautiful interface
  • Verified profiles
  • Audio & video calls
  • Stories
  • Private photo albums
  • Profile recommendations
  • Comments & recommendations
  • Join as a single or as a couple
TYPE 3

Trends / Humour

Use the language and editing styles currently found in short-form video, while staying aligned with Nouslib’s premium and welcoming brand identity.

  • Trending audio
  • Text-to-speech
  • Funny situations
  • Fast editing
  • Relatable moments
TYPE 4

Street Interview

Dynamic social interviews asking simple relationship questions, e.g.:

  • “What’s the biggest green flag on a dating app?”
  • “Would you ever join a dating app as a couple?”
  • “What’s the first thing you look for on someone’s profile?”

05

Script Structure

Every video should follow this Hook · Body · CTA structure.

0–3 sec

Hook

Objective: stop users from scrolling immediately.

  • Strong opening sentence
  • Dynamic visual
  • On-screen text
  • “I thought every dating app was exactly the same…”
  • “Looking for something different?”
  • “This dating app surprised me…”
    • 3–25 sec

    Body

    Objective: present the problem and explain why Nouslib is the solution.

    • Fast pacing
    • Cut every 2–3 seconds
    • Dynamic subtitles
    • Native short-form editing style
    Final sec

    CTA

    Objective: encourage users to download the app.

  • “Download Nouslib today.”
  • “Discover a new way to meet people.”
  • “Join the Nouslib community.”

    • 06

    Deliverables

    For every approved concept, please send videos that meet the following:

    • Final edited video
    • If possible, hook variations for A/B testing — the number of variations to be specified by us
    • Consistent use of Nouslib illustrations and brand colors
    • Brand subtitles matching our visual identity
    • Respect for platform safe areas for captions and text placement

    Integrate our visual assets (logo, illustrations and graphic elements) whenever appropriate to reinforce brand recognition.
