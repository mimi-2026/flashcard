What helps most

  1. Follow the question groups (A–E) above — even "not asked" or "need to follow up" is useful, since it maps straight onto the tracker fields.
  2. Call out concrete numbers on their own line — headcount, ¥ amounts, day/week counts. These are what update Pricing/Delivery directly; buried in a paragraph they're easy to miss.
  3. Separate decisions from open items — what got agreed on the call vs. what's still pending a follow-up either side.
  4. Note next steps with an owner — "MISM to send X by Friday" / "we send the brief by Y" — this becomes the new Next Step.
  5. Flag who said what when it matters (MISM/Noguchi vs. something Bianca or Renzo raised live) — the tracker keeps per-speaker attribution.
  6. Any new links or files shared during the call (decks, sample scripts, contracts).
  7. Your gut read on moving forward — this becomes the status/action-owner call.
